Sites

A site represents one plant. Multiple plants are supported — each has its own shifts, modules, and absence codes.

Shifts + breaks

Each shift has clock start/end, billing hours (Mon–Thu and Fri may differ), and break slots (paid or unpaid).

Breaks

Modules (supervisor + line grouping)

A module is one supervisor's responsibility. It maps one shift to a group of assembly lines (e.g., "ASSY CELL 1,4,5").

Absence codes

Codes used on the attendance sheet (U, V, DI, PP, P, PT, SI per the pilot spec). Seed the defaults once per site, then edit/extend as needed.
